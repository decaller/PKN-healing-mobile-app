## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a dignified, executive spiritual interface tailored for Islamic leaders, character mentors, and discerning parents. The experience synthesizes contemplative depth with institutional rigor—bridging classical Islamic pedagogy (Pendidikan Karakter Nabawiyah) and contemporary behavioral healing.

The aesthetic philosophy merges **Architectural Slate Minimalism** with **Subtle Spiritual Illumination**. Rather than relying on superficial ornament, the system projects quiet authority through obsidian-grade surfaces, disciplined typography, precise metrics, and localized luminescence:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, contemplative, clinically precise, deeply rooted in Nabawi ethics.
- **Atmosphere:** An executive sanctuary. A deep pitch-midnight canvas anchored by structural slate panels, punctuated by measured emerald life-force and subdued antique gold accents.
- **Density Paradigm:** High information density balanced by rigorous structural cadence, intentional micro-spacing, and typographic air that prevents cognitive fatigue during intensive daily evaluations and reflective counseling.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive nocturnal environment with high-contrast functional hierarchy and chromatic spiritual resonance.

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F14`):** Pitch Midnight. Absorbs optical glare and frames foreground layers with contemplative depth.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#151D24`):** Deep Slate Primary. Structural card containers, bottom navigation sheets, and persistent panels.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1E293B`):** Deep Slate Secondary. Elevated interaction targets, modal surfaces, and data-dense segmented clusters.
- **Surface Stroke (`#334155` at 50% opacity):** Structural hairline boundary framing all containers.

### Chromatic Accents
- **Emerald Glow (`#14B8A6`):** Primary action token, progression milestone, and spiritual vitality marker. Applied with disciplined restraint across key buttons, active indicators, and luminous boundary highlights.
- **Antique Nabawi Gold (`#EAB308`):** Sacred distinction token. Reserved exclusively for sacred ayat, Hadith classifications, wisdom bookmarks, and leadership credential tags.
- **Celestial Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`):** Analytical clarity token. Drives secondary metrics, behavioral analytics curves, introspective prompts, and informational indicators.

### Text & Semantic Roles
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Directives, primary headlines, active metrics.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Explanatory guidance, diagnostic metadata, Nabawi context.
- **Text Tertiary (`#64748B`):** Timestamps, deactivated states, footnote citations.
- **Emerald Glow Stroke (`rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.35)`): Radiance state applied to active or healing-focused modules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy accommodates dual linguistic traditions: modern executive data delivery via `Inter` alongside authentic classical Arabic calligraphy via `Amiri`.

- **Inter:** Serves as the primary operational engine. Rendered with calibrated tabular tracking for diagnostic scores, clinical metrics, character progress reports, and action hierarchies.
- **Amiri:** Preserved strictly for sacred transmissions—Qur'anic verses, Nabawi sayings, and foundational prophetic aphorisms. The line height is intentionally expanded to `48px` and `38px` to preserve diacritical clarity (*tashkeel*) without vertical collision.
- **Typographic Rules:**
  - Sacred Arabic quotations must consistently sit right-to-left within an elevated slate enclosure, followed immediately by left-to-right translation set in `body-sm` (`#94A3B8`).
  - Metric displays use `Inter` at `700` weight with `-0.02em` letter tracking to maintain density without visual noise.

## Layout & Spacing

Designed specifically for mobile-first handheld workflows, optimizing for one-thumb reachability across executive dashboards, evaluation cards, and reflective reading streams.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 4-column fluid mobile grid anchored by an outer margin of `1.25rem` (20px) and column gutters of `1rem` (16px).
- **Rhythm:** Strict 4px/8px modular base. Compact vertical gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) group related diagnostic metrics, while deliberate macro-gaps (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) delineate spiritual reflection modules from tactical tracking components.
- **Safe Area Insets:** Fixed bottom action bar containers enforce a minimum clearance of `34px` on modern notch/home-indicator viewports, preventing mis-taps during deep navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **Tonal Layering** and **Controlled Luminescence**, avoiding heavy drop shadows that muddy deep pitch surfaces.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0B0F14`. Non-interactive backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Structural Modules):** `#151D24` combined with a 1px border of `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`. Flat elevation with no ambient shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active/Floating Elements):** `#1E293B` with a dual-layer profile:
  - Perimeter Hairline: `1px solid rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.4)`
  - Diffused Glow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`
- **Sacred Card Elevation:** Elevated `#151D24` panel encased by a subtle Antique Gold accent outline (`1px solid rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.3)`), projecting quiet sanctity without visual glare.

## Shapes

The design system employs precise architectural roundedness that projects contemporary stability:
- Base controls, diagnostic tags, and input fields use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Standard parent cards, metric tiles, and bottom sheets use `1rem` (16px).
- Modals, prominent sacred quote containers, and diagnostic progress bars use `1.5rem` (24px).
- Fully pill-shaped geometry (`9999px`) is reserved exclusively for status badges, role indicators, and compact tracker chips.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Executive Action:** Emerald solid background (`#14B8A6`), text `#0B0F14` (`600` weight). Active states introduce an internal glow and subtle scale compression (0.98).
- **Secondary Action:** Slate background (`#1E293B`), text `#F8FAFC`, framed by a 1px border (`#334155`).
- **Sacred Reflection Action:** Pitch slate surface with Antique Gold border (`#EAB308` at 60), text `#EAB308`.

### Sacred Quote Cards (*Hikmah Modules*)
- **Background:** Deep Slate Primary (`#151D24`) with an organic gradient wash (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.05) 0%, rgba(21, 29, 36, 1) 100%)`).
- **Typography:** Centered or right-aligned `Amiri` at `20px` or `26px`, followed by an ornamental hairline separator (`#334155`), ending with translation and source citation in `Inter` (`#94A3B8`).

### Diagnostic & Metric Chips
- **Structure:** Height of 28px, rounded pill border (`9999px`), background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)`.
- **States:**
  - *Healing Active:* Text `#14B8A6`, left indicator dot `#14B8A6` with a 4px soft bloom.
  - *Character Focus:* Text `#EAB308`, border `rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.4)`.
  - *Analytical Observation:* Text `#38BDF8`, border `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`.

### Lists & Tracking Rows
- **Layout:** High-density, horizontal split layout. Key title and timestamp aligned left; Nabawi category tag and score indicator aligned right.
- **Divider:** 1px hairline (`rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.3)`), indented `1rem` to preserve visual continuity.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Darkened slate (`#0F151B`), 1px structural stroke (`#334155`).
- **Focus State:** 1px border in Emerald Glow (`#14B8A6`) with an ambient external blur of `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.2)`. Label transitions to `label-xs` in `#14B8A6`.

### Parental & Healing Progress Bar
- **Track:** 6px height, rounded radius (`9999px`), fill `#151D24`.
- **Indicator:** Smooth gradient transition from `#38BDF8` (Observation) to `#14B8A6` (Maturity/Healing), emitting a faint localized glow at the leading edge.
