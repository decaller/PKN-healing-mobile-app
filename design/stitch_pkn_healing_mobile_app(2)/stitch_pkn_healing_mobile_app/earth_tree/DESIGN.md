## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in quiet permanence, intentionality, and biological balance. Drawing inspiration from ancient arbor structures rooted deep within mineral-dense granite beneath clear open skies, the visual atmosphere balances solid tactile weight with boundless open air. The experience avoids sterile corporate uniformity and hyper-synthetic tech conventions in favor of grounded authenticity, patient pacing, and contemplative clarity.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges **Organic Tactility** with **Serene Editorial Modernism**. Components convey subtle physical substance—reminiscent of honed river stone, fine fibrous botanical paper, and sun-warmed slate. Surfaces feature soft, natural edge treatment and low-strain chromatic contrasts designed to foster focus, mental calm, and mindful interaction across extended digital sessions.

## Colors

The palette establishes a dialogue between terrestrial foundations and open horizons. Every hue is drawn from natural environmental phenomena: unpolluted sky, photosynthetic chlorophyll, sun-baked clay, and weathered bedrock.

### Functional Roles & Contrast Architecture
- **Primary (`#0284C7` - Celestial Sky):** Used for focal anchors, key interactive states, active indicators, and high-priority navigations. It acts as the clearing canopy overhead, offering instant cognitive orientation.
- **Secondary (`#15803D` - Meadow Canopy):** Anchors constructive actions, positive affirmations, progression markers, and organic accents. It reinforces vitality, resilience, and balance.
- **Tertiary (`#9A6A38` - Ancient Bark / Amber Earth):** Functions as an authentic tactile accent for metadata, highlights, subtle framing, and specialized tags, adding depth to cold technical screens.
- **Neutral Core (`#76746D` - Weathered Stone):** Used for balanced optical framing, hairline borders, and balanced secondary text across light and dark modes.

### Surface Color Logic
- **Light Mode Foundations:** Surface canvas rests on warm sand-ecru (`#F7F5EE`), stepping progressively upward into soft pressed linen (`#EDE8DC`) and milled limestone (`#E3DCCB`) for card tiers and nested elements. Text relies on deep peat umber (`#1C1917`) to preserve softness while ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility.
- **Dark Mode Foundations:** Surfaces reverse into deep igneous basalt (`#161A1D`), mineral slate (`#1E2328`), and weathered granite (`#262D34`). Contrast remains gentle on the eyes, utilizing warm chalk (`#EFECE6`) and misty bone (`#D3CEC4`) for typography instead of pure piercing white.

## Typography

The typographical pairing pairs deliberate ancestral craft with modern digital ergonomics. 

- **Display & Headings (Merriweather):** Chosen for its sturdy serifs, warm letterforms, and literary weight. It instills narrative gravity, patience, and authoritative peace across viewports.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides an open, geometric yet humanist counter-balance. Its clean counters and balanced x-height ensure scan-efficiency, comfortable long-form reading, and pristine button/input legibility.

### Hierarchy & Typesetting Guidelines
1. **Vertical Rhythm:** Heading levels should preserve generous spacing before next paragraph blocks, avoiding compressed title stacks.
2. **Editorial Restraint:** Limit high-weight serifs (`700`) strictly to titles of structural importance (`display-lg`, `headline-xl`, `headline-md`). Section leads and chapter titles should utilize regular weight (`400`) serifs for a classic, unhurried literary feel.
3. **Labels & Microcopy:** `label-sm` utilizes an expanded tracking (`0.04em`) to maintain legibility when set in uppercase or pill containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model honors negative space as a functional design element, echoing open plains and tranquil natural landscapes. 

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with max canvas bound of `1280px`, fluid scaling, `3rem` (`48px`) outer margin, and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` (`32px`) outer margin and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters.
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Content cascades strictly in a single column or 2-column card arrays.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Spacing follows an intentional rhythmic cadence based on structural increments rather than strict, mechanical 8px blocks.
- Component-interior padding scales logically: `space-xs` (6px) for micro badges and chips; `space-sm` (12px) for inputs and tight cells; `space-md` (20px) for regular card interiors; `space-lg` (32px) for major card sections; and `space-xl` (56px) for spacious thematic dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **Tonal Masonry and Ambient Sedimentary Layers** rather than stark drop shadows. The design system rejects cold artificial drop shadows in favor of subtle surface stepping and diffused soil warmth.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Bedrock Canvas):** The root background (`#F7F5EE` in light mode, `#161A1D` in dark mode). Serves as the continuous foundational plane.
- **Layer 1 (Carved Surface / Cards):** Raised one tone (`#EDE8DC` in light mode, `#1E2328` in dark mode) and bounded by a low-opacity tactile border: `1px solid rgba(118, 116, 109, 0.18)`. An ultra-diffuse ambient shadow is applied: `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Stones / Popovers & Menus):** Further elevated (`#E3DCCB` in light, `#262D34` in dark) with a soft ambient aura: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(35, 30, 27, 0.08)`, paired with a delicate rim border: `1px solid rgba(118, 116, 109, 0.28)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modal Monoliths):** Maximum depth layer featuring a 24px ambient blur behind the viewport scrim (`rgba(22, 26, 29, 0.65)` in both light and dark mode) to emulate focused, shaded contemplation.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses the soft wear of water over stone over centuries. Forms are neither harsh and angular (brutalist) nor completely synthetic or pill-stretched (juvenile neo-modern). 

- Base components (inputs, list items, small buttons) feature a standard `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius.
- Larger interactive elements, content cards, and modal sheets scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`), giving structural surfaces a distinct river-pebble roundness.
- Specialized indicators, chips, and notification dots retain circular or full capsule rounding (`9999px`) to visually separate continuous textual containers from dynamic operational tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Sky Horizon):** Rich sky-blue background (`#0284C7`), crisp warm-bone text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` corner radius, with subtle inset highlight border on top (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). On hover, color transitions smoothly to `#0369A1` with an expansion of soft ambient shadow.
- **Secondary (Forest Canopy):** Meadow green background (`#15803D`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), providing clear alternative actions such as "Save", "Grow", or "Commit".
- **Tertiary / Outlined (Stone Edge):** Transparent base with a 1px border using Neutral Core (`rgba(118, 116, 109, 0.3)`), dark slate/umber text, shifting to a soft sand fill on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact height (28px-32px), `9999px` capsule radius.
- Micro-padded with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal spacing.
- Earth-toned semantic tints: bark brown (`#9A6A38`) for thematic filters, moss green for online/active state, and pale mist blue for informational notes. Zero neon saturation.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Bound by a continuous `1.5px` border in stone-neutral (`#D3CEC4` in light, `#38404A` in dark). Inset background uses the deepest tier surface. Focused states highlight with a soft Sky Blue border ring (`#0284C7`) and a 3px diffused outer halo at 15% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic squares (`4px` radius for checkboxes; full circles for radios). In unchecked state, they mirror hollow stone wells. When checked, they fill smoothly with Meadow Green (`#15803D`) featuring an organic, off-center check tick or soft interior stone pip.

### Cards
- Surface utilizes Layer 1 stone-paper tone (`#EDE8DC` light, `#1E2328` dark) encased in a refined `1px` mineral border. 
- Padding maintains generous breathing room (`space-md` to `space-lg`). 
- Interactive cards elevate gently by `-2px` on the Y-axis on hover, softening their border toward Sky Blue.

### Lists & Dividers
- List items feature generous line heights with minimum 48px touch targets.
- Separators do not use harsh vector rules; they use low-contrast natural dividers (`rgba(118, 116, 109, 0.12)`) that fade out subtly at the screen edge.

### Specialized Component: The Grounding Banner
- A quiet announcement and contextual guidance panel featuring an asymmetric curved border (`rounded-lg`), a warm pale earth wash, and an understated leaf or stone icon in secondary grass green. Designed to deliver mindful alerts without alarming the user.
